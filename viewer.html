Tap a FAD marker or any spot on the map for coordinates. Scroll or pinch to zoom, drag to move.
Maps: NOAA CoastWatch, JPL MUR, NOAA RTOFS. Gold markers are FAD buoys (toggle with the FADs button). Seas and wind: NDBC buoys, NWS Mobile marine zones, and an Open-Meteo model table (not for navigation). Solunar for Perdido Pass, CT: majors = moon overhead/underfoot (2 h), minors = moonrise/moonset (1 h); 7-day strip shows start times. Values at a tapped spot come from the same data grids the maps are drawn from. Experimental, not for navigation. Updated each morning.
